Best Things To Do in Boone NC This Weekend

Andrew Plyler, REALTOR® · September 25, 2026 · Boone, NC

Fall in the High Country Is Something Special

If you've never spent a fall weekend in Boone, NC, let me tell you — you're missing one of the most genuinely beautiful seasonal experiences in the entire Southeast. The elevation here sits around 3,300 feet, which means the colors arrive earlier, the air gets crisp sooner, and the whole town takes on a kind of golden-hour quality that's hard to describe without just being here. As someone who grew up visiting this area as a kid and eventually made it my permanent home in 2020, I can say without hesitation that late September in the High Country is as good as it gets.

Halloween is just 36 days away, which means the mountains are in full transition mode — pumpkins on every porch along King Street, hayrides at local farms, and that unmistakable smell of wood smoke drifting through the evening air. Whether you're visiting for the first time or you've been coming to these mountains for years, this weekend offers a little something for everyone.

What's Happening This Weekend in Boone and the Surrounding Area

The fall festival season is in full swing across the High Country, and there's plenty to keep you busy from Friday evening through Sunday afternoon.

  • Farmers Markets: The Watauga County Farmers Market on Depot Street runs Saturday mornings and is one of the best in the region right now. You'll find local apple varieties, fresh cider, winter squash, handmade goods, and vendors who have been part of this community for decades. It's the kind of place that reminds you why small-town living matters.
  • Valle Crucis Community Park: Valle Crucis — where my family has had a home since 1978 — is absolutely stunning this time of year. The park along the Watauga River is a perfect spot for a morning walk, a picnic, or just sitting on the bank and watching the water move. The Mast General Store, just a short drive away on Broadstone Road, is a must-visit for anyone new to the area.
  • Blue Ridge Parkway Leaf Peeping: The Parkway access points near Boone, including the Price Park area at Milepost 296, are seeing peak early-fall color this week. Pull-offs along the Parkway offer some of the most dramatic ridge-line views in the eastern United States, and right now they are absolutely worth the drive.
  • Appalachian State Football: App State is home this fall, and game weekends bring a contagious energy to downtown Boone. Even if you're not a Mountaineers fan, the atmosphere around King Street and the Kidd Brewer Stadium area on game day is a uniquely Boone experience.
  • Hiking: Rough Ridge and the Tanawha Trail offer accessible, rewarding hikes with payoff views that punch well above their difficulty level. Beacon Heights, just off the Parkway near Grandfather Mountain, is another short hike with outsized scenery — great for families or anyone who wants maximum impact with moderate effort.

Where to Eat and Drink This Weekend

Boone's dining scene has grown significantly over the last several years, and fall weekends are when local restaurants really hit their stride. Vidalia on Depot Street remains one of the best dinner spots in town — creative, locally sourced, and reliably excellent. For a more casual afternoon, Booneshine Brewing is a great place to grab a pint after a hike and watch the mountains do their thing through the big windows. If you're heading toward Banner Elk or Sugar Mountain for the afternoon, Sorrento's and several spots along Tynecastle Highway are worth the detour.

And if you're in Valle Crucis on Saturday morning, there is truly no better breakfast than grabbing something from one of the local spots near the Mast Store and eating it outside. Simple, honest, and completely unhurried — which is kind of the whole point.

Why Fall Weekends Are the Best Time to Explore Boone NC Real Estate

Here's something I tell every buyer who visits this time of year: what you're feeling right now — that pull toward the mountains, the sense that this place could be home — that feeling is real, and it's worth paying attention to.

The Boone NC real estate market remains active and competitive heading into fall 2026. Mountain property in NC continues to attract buyers from across the Carolinas, Tennessee, Georgia, and beyond, drawn by the climate, the quality of life, and yes, the investment potential. Inventory has remained tight in many price ranges, which means well-priced properties — whether you're looking at a downtown condo, a Valle Crucis farmhouse, or wooded acreage on a ridge — tend to move with purpose.

For families exploring Appalachian State housing options, the neighborhoods closest to campus offer a range of price points and property types, from smaller cottages to larger homes with rental potential. If you're thinking about whether to buy a home in Boone NC, a fall weekend visit is one of the best research tools available to you — because you get to see the town at its most alive, its most itself.

As a High Country REALTOR who was born here, went to school here, and chose to come back here for good, I bring a perspective to this market that goes beyond comparable sales and days on market. I know these roads, these neighborhoods, and these communities in a way that takes years to build.
