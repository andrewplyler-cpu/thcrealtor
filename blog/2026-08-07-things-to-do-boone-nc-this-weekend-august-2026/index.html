Best Things To Do in Boone NC This Weekend

Andrew Plyler, REALTOR® · August 07, 2026 · Boone, NC

August in the High Country: Why This Weekend Is Worth the Drive

There's a reason people visit Boone, NC once and start daydreaming about a second home before they've even checked out of their rental. August here is something special — temperatures that rarely crack 80 degrees, mountain ridgelines wearing their deepest shade of green, and a calendar full of local events that feel genuinely rooted in this community. With Labor Day weekend only 25 days away, the next few weekends represent the sweet spot of High Country summer: busy enough to feel alive, but not yet the full autumn rush that transforms the region every October.

I was born here, went to school at App State, and my family has had a home in Valle Crucis since 1978. I've watched this area evolve through every season for decades. August has always been one of my favorites — and this weekend is a great example of why.

Weekend Events and Local Happenings Right Now

Downtown Boone's King Street is the heartbeat of the High Country on any given weekend, but summer Saturdays take it to another level. Local vendors, live music drifting out of open storefronts, and foot traffic that reflects just how much demand there is for mountain living — it all comes together in a way that's hard to replicate anywhere else in North Carolina.

The High Country Farmers Market continues its summer run this weekend, offering locally grown produce, handmade goods, and the kind of community energy that reminds you why people choose to put down roots here. If you haven't been, it's an easy stop and a genuine slice of what daily life looks like for High Country residents.

For those who enjoy the arts, Appalachian Theatre on King Street continues its summer programming — check their current schedule for film screenings, live performances, and community events. The theatre is one of Boone's most beloved landmarks and a cornerstone of downtown's cultural identity.

Over in Valle Crucis, the legendary Mast General Store remains an anchor of the community that my family has loved since long before it became a regional destination. It's worth a visit any time of year, but summer weekends on that stretch of Broadstone Road feel timeless in the best way.

Outdoor Activities: Make the Most of the Mountain Air

If you're spending the weekend outdoors — and you absolutely should be — the High Country delivers in every direction. Here are some of the best ways to experience the landscape this time of year:

  • Grandfather Mountain offers hiking trails at multiple skill levels, the famous Mile High Swinging Bridge, and views that never get old regardless of how many times you've made the drive up.
  • Price Lake at Julian Price Memorial Park along the Blue Ridge Parkway is perfect for a morning kayak, a lakeside picnic, or a gentle trail walk with the family.
  • Rough Ridge Trail near the Parkway gives hikers an exposed ridgeline scramble with panoramic payoff — one of the most rewarding short hikes in the region.
  • New River State Park is ideal for tubing, canoeing, or simply sitting on the bank of one of the oldest rivers in North America on a warm August afternoon.
  • Cycling the Blue Ridge Parkway on an early Saturday or Sunday morning, before the scenic traffic picks up, is a bucket-list experience for road cyclists.

All of this — the trails, the rivers, the farms, the small towns — is accessible within 20 to 30 minutes of downtown Boone. That's not a selling point I manufacture. It's just the geography of living here.

Dining and Drinking: Eat and Drink Local This Weekend

Boone's food and beverage scene has grown impressively over the past several years, and a summer weekend is a great time to explore it. Booneshine Brewing Company on Blowing Rock Road remains a local staple, with a patio that earns its reputation on a warm August evening. Stick Boy Bread Co. on King Street is an institution for breakfast and pastries — arrive early, because the good stuff goes fast.

For dinner, the options continue to expand. Whether you're in the mood for farm-to-table dining, wood-fired pizza, or a classic mountain tavern atmosphere, King Street and its surrounding blocks offer more than enough to fill a weekend itinerary without repeating yourself.

Thinking About More Than Just the Weekend? Here's What the Market Looks Like

A lot of the people who come up for a weekend event or a quick summer trip find themselves doing something they didn't plan on: driving through neighborhoods, peeking at listings on their phones, and asking the quiet question of whether owning something up here might actually be possible.

If that sounds familiar, I'll tell you honestly — Boone NC real estate continues to attract serious interest, and inventory, while it has shown some movement, remains competitive for well-priced properties in desirable areas. Whether you're exploring a primary residence, a mountain property in NC for weekends and holidays, or thinking ahead to the Appalachian State housing market as a parent or investor, the High Country offers a range of price points and property types that often surprises first-time buyers.

As a High Country REALTOR who has personal roots here going back generations, I bring context to these conversations that goes well beyond the MLS. I know the neighborhoods, the roads in winter, the HOA reputations, the flood zones, and the views worth paying for. If you're starting to wonder whether you should buy a home in Boone NC, this weekend might be the best research trip you'll ever take.

Enjoy the mountain air this weekend. And when you're ready to talk about making it a little more permanent — I'm easy to reach.
