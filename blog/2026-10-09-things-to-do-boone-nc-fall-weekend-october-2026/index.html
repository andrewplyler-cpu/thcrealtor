Best Things To Do in Boone NC This Weekend | Fall 2026

Andrew Plyler, REALTOR® · October 09, 2026 · Boone, NC

October in the High Country: There's No Better Time to Be Here

I'll be honest with you — October in Boone, NC is the reason people fall in love with this place and never leave. I've watched it happen dozens of times. Someone drives up for a leaf-peeping weekend, parks on King Street, grabs a coffee, walks around for two hours, and by Sunday afternoon they're texting me asking what homes are available. It's that kind of place. It's that kind of month.

With Halloween just 22 days away, the High Country is fully in its autumn element right now. The ridgelines along the Blue Ridge Parkway are painted in deep burgundy, burnt orange, and amber gold. Temperatures are crisp in the mornings — think mid-40s at dawn — and cool and comfortable through the afternoons. If you've been considering a visit, or if you've been on the fence about exploring Boone NC real estate, this weekend is your sign to make the trip.

What's Happening This Weekend in Boone and the Surrounding Area

The High Country packs its calendar in October, and this weekend is no exception. Here's a look at what's going on and where to point yourself:

  • Downtown Boone Fall Market: The grassy corridor near Depot Street draws local vendors, artisan crafters, and small-batch food producers every October weekend. It's casual, it's community-driven, and it's a great way to get a feel for the culture of the town beyond the tourist surface.
  • Blue Ridge Parkway Foliage Drives: Milepost 291 near Blowing Rock gives you some of the most accessible and jaw-dropping views of the season. Pull-offs are plentiful, and the drive north toward the Linn Cove Viaduct is genuinely one of the most beautiful roads in America right now.
  • Valle Crucis Community Park and the Mast General Store: If you haven't made the short drive out NC-105 to Valle Crucis, this weekend is the time. My family has had a home out there since 1978, so I may be a little biased — but the valley in October is something special. Stop into the original Mast General Store, pick up something warm, and just slow down for a minute. It's the antidote to everything hectic.
  • Appalachian State Football: App State is playing at home this weekend at Kidd Brewer Stadium. Game day in Boone is an experience all its own. The energy on campus, the tailgates along Rivers Street, the crowd filling the stadium — it's electric. If you've been curious about Appalachian State housing and the lifestyle that surrounds campus, walking around on a game day weekend gives you a real sense of the rhythm of this college town.
  • Halloween Events Ramp Up: With the holiday just around the corner, look for pumpkin patches near Banner Elk, haunted attractions in the area, and family-friendly fall festivals across Watauga and Avery counties. The region goes all-in for Halloween in the most charming way possible.

Where to Eat and Drink This Weekend

Boone's dining scene has matured significantly over the past several years, and fall is when its coziest spots really shine. Proper on Howard Street is a local favorite for craft cocktails and shareable plates — go early or expect a wait on a Saturday night. Dan'l Boone Inn on Hardin Street is an institution: family-style Southern cooking that's been feeding visitors and locals alike for decades. If you want something more casual after a long hike, Appalachian Mountain Brewery downtown has great beer and a crowd that tells you exactly what kind of town Boone is — friendly, unpretentious, and proud of where it comes from.

Why Fall Visitors Often Become High Country Buyers

Here's something I've seen play out more times than I can count in my years as a High Country REALTOR: the people who visit in October are often the people who call me in November. There's something about experiencing this area at its most beautiful that shifts the internal conversation from someday to what would it actually take?

The Boone NC real estate market remains active and competitive heading into the fall. Inventory of well-priced mountain property in NC — whether that's a cozy cabin, a second home in a ski community near Banner Elk, or a buildable lot with long-range views — tends to tighten as the year closes out. Buyers who are serious about positioning themselves for a spring purchase are often doing their research and making introductions right now, in October, before the holiday slowdown.

If you've thought about what it would mean to buy a home in Boone NC — as a primary residence, a second home, or a mountain retreat — the fall is genuinely the best time to start that conversation. Not because of pressure, but because of clarity. When you see this place in October, you understand it differently.

Ready to Explore the High Country — and What It Could Mean for You?

I know this area the way you only know a place when you've loved it across decades — the neighborhoods, the back roads, the valleys, the views, and the communities that make the High Country genuinely special.
